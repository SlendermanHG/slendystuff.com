# SlendyStuffClicker v0.5.2.7

A SlendyStuff Production · Free Windows preview · One portable EXE

More ways to collect, plan and play, with a refreshed Gameplay window and updates you approve inside the app.

## What's new

- **Refreshed Gameplay settings:** grouped sidebar navigation replaces the crowded tab strip. All sixteen pages remain available, with dark dropdowns, clearer tables, padded status panels and separate spell/sales cards. Achievement details have more room, overlapping fields are corrected, and live status updates preserve your reading position.
- **Approve portable updates:** Check for updates shows the newer version and release notes. Choose Update this copy to approve downloading and replacing the EXE. The app verifies the expected GitHub asset size, SHA-256 and embedded versions, retains the previous EXE for recovery, and restarts stopped. Profiles and settings are kept.
- **Seasonal collection:** collectors follow native rewards across Valentine's hearts, Easter bunnies, Business Day contracts, golden-cookie appearances and cookie storms. Christmas reindeer have their own toggle. Halloween spooky cookies use the separate wrath-cookie option; their outcome is unknown before collection.
- **Combo assistance:** optionally wait for Frenzy, or Frenzy plus a building bonus, before casting Force the Hand of Fate. Set remaining-time, mana and backfire limits, and optionally click only during click bonuses. Click Frenzy and Dragonflight are recognized; other automatic spending pauses during combo windows.
- **Optional Godzamok sales:** separately enable limited sales, select a building, choose up to 100 buildings per batch, set a retained minimum and limit batches per run. Godzamok must already be slotted. Temple, Wizard towers and buildings with their own active building bonus are protected. The used allowance carries through game restarts; repurchase remains manual.
- **More minigame controls:** optional sugar-lump harvesting, mature missing-seed harvesting, Garden planting and soil/freeze previews, spell assistance, Pantheon changes, individual sale previews and budgeted Stock Market trading. Rare, immortal and already-unlocked plants are protected from automatic harvesting. Destructive actions and sugar-lump spending require individual review.
- **More Completionist guidance:** supported lump, Garden, spell and Stock Market milestones join the achievement browser. Assistance respects enabled controls, reserves and conflicting goals. Unknown requirements remain for manual play; the app never awards achievements directly.
- **Scheduled maintenance:** optionally save, gracefully close, reopen through Steam and resume, with up to ten private save backups. Both options default off. A failed save, backup or restart leaves automation stopped.

## Updating and setup

If you use v0.5.2.5 or earlier, download this EXE, close the clicker and replace your old copy. In v0.5.2.7, future updates can be approved from Check for updates.

Choose Update companion or Set up game, then fully restart Cookie Clicker and enable SlendyStuffClicker in Options → Manage mods. Back up your game save before enabling sacrifice, ascension or other destructive options. Existing local profiles and pairing settings are retained.

* This portable preview supports the Steam edition on 64-bit Windows 10/11 and game version 2.053. It has no installer; setup adds the companion mod to the game folder. The EXE is unsigned, so Windows may warn or block it. Checksums identify the downloaded file; they do not provide publisher signing or a Windows trust exemption.

* Save and fully restart both the game and clicker at least every twelve hours, or sooner if memory grows or performance slows. Restarting the clicker alone does not release game memory. Resource guards and scheduled maintenance reduce risk but do not guarantee unattended stability.

* Spell outcomes remain random. Combo assistance does not predict results, reload saves, refill mana with sugar lumps, sell Wizard towers or change dragon auras. Sales, sacrifices and ascension can remove progress; enable them only when you accept their effects. Steam achievement delivery and long-session stability can vary.
