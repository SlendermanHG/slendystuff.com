Free portable Windows preview for Cookie Clicker on Steam. Its companion mod sends native game actions while the system mouse stays free. Features include independent controls, golden-cookie and seasonal reward collection, optional wrath/reindeer collection, selected shop buying, targets and reserves, wrinkler rules, seasonal progress, Completionist guidance and saved profiles. Auto-buy and assisted gameplay options are off until enabled. Browser/mobile editions are unsupported.

The setup video was recorded with v0.5.1.0. It uses genuine app screenshots and labeled synthetic actions; it is not live Steam footage.

* Independent SlendyStuff production, not affiliated with or endorsed by the game or competing-tool developers.
* Unsigned Windows preview; devices may warn or block it. Updates are manual.
* Plan to save and fully restart both apps at least every 12 hours, or sooner if memory grows or performance slows. Resource checks do not guarantee unattended stability. Earlier EXEs may display a six-hour reminder.
* Steam achievement delivery and representative long-session reliability are not certified by isolated tests.
