Free portable Windows preview for Cookie Clicker on Steam. Uses a companion mod to send game actions, leaving the system mouse free. Supports optional normal golden-cookie collection and selected building/upgrade auto-buy with a reserve. Auto-buy is off by default. Browser/mobile versions are unsupported.

The video uses genuine app screenshots and labeled synthetic game actions, not live Steam footage.

Disclosure: an independent SlendyStuff production, not endorsed by the game developers or OP Auto Clicker. Unsigned preview; Windows may warn or block it. Restart the game and clicker at least every six hours. A full live Steam restart cycle and overnight stability remain unverified. No paid review or endorsement is requested.
